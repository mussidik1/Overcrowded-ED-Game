## Rejected findings

**R6-03 (epinephrine in VF arrest scored as harm).** The answer-key data is as described, and the harm toast appears if an order sheet is forced onto an arrest patient. No player can reach that state. An arriving arrest is handled as a `code` task: tapping "Run the code" queues it (engine.js:1433), and when it ends `taskDone` calls `codeOutcome` directly (engine.js:1511), never `awaitOrders`. A patient of yours who arrests later is refused with "See the patient first" (`signOrders`, engine.js:1763); in `v9.js` 0 of 30 attempts signed. The reviewer's reproduction called `OC.awaitOrders` on an arrest patient by hand. The real clinical problem, a VF arrest with no defibrillation, drugs or rhythm decisions, is R6-04. The `tr: []` data should still be fixed before any order sheet is ever opened for an arrest.

**R6-10 (opioid re-sedation crash only adds oxygen).** `CRASH.od` exists, but `crash()` runs only for ESI 2 patients without an active alert who are waiting in triage or the waiting room (engine.js:3222, 3226). The `od` case is ESI 1 (engine.js:627), and nothing in the engine lowers `p.esi` (it is only ever set to 1, engine.js:3143, 3179). So the overdose never crashes in play. The reviewer reached it through the `crashNow` test hook. The same reasoning removes the crash part of R6-11 (anaphylaxis is ESI 1 too), but the rest of R6-11 stands.

## Needs Clinician Confirmation

**R6-17 (TIA with ABCD2 0-3 graded "home").** Question for the clinician: *For a TIA patient whose symptoms have resolved, with a normal non-contrast CT head, normal ECG and normal labs, and an ABCD2 of 0-3, but no CTA or MRI done in the ED, should a teaching game score discharge home with rapid TIA-clinic follow-up as the single right answer, or should observation for vascular imaging be the right answer (or at least accepted as equally right)?* Note that today the game accepts no alternative to "home" in this band (engine.js:1151, `ok` is empty).

Two supplementary questions inside Confirmed findings, where the main finding stands on other grounds:

- **R6-08:** *In suspected STEMI or NSTEMI in the ED, should morphine for ongoing pain and an early beta-blocker be scored as "wrong drug" (penalised), as neutral add-ons, or as correct?* Nitroglycerin being penalised is clearly wrong and is enough to confirm the finding; morphine (Class IIb, observational harm signals) and early IV beta-blockade (harm in shock-risk patients per COMMIT) are judgment calls.
- **R6-15:** *In a DKA case where the potassium will be 4.8, is it acceptable to teach ordering the insulin drip on the same signature as the BMP, as real order sets do with insulin started after the result, or must the game hold insulin until the potassium is back?* The potassium-replacement half (ADA 2024: add potassium at K 3.5-5.0) is not in question.
